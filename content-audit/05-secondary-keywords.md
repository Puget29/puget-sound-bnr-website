## Findings

- **Strong, uniform coverage on every page** — there are **no pages missing** kitchen or bathroom coverage,
  and none with a lopsided balance. By the "present and balanced" test, all 81 **Pass**.
- **But coverage is shallow and 100% templated.** The same ~116 words appear on all 81 pages with only the
  city swapped. There is **zero city-specific** kitchen or bathroom substance (no local kitchen/bath
  considerations, no neighborhood examples, no local style/material notes).
- Scored **Partial** sitewide: minimum satisfied, secondary search intent only lightly served.

## Pages with strong vs weak secondary coverage

Because the section is identical everywhere, **no page is stronger or weaker than any other** on secondary
coverage — they are all the same Partial. This uniformity is itself the finding: the opportunity to make
kitchen/bathroom coverage *locally distinct* (e.g. tying it to the housing stock named in `local_body`) is
unused on all 81 pages.

## Recommendation (summary — detail in `09`)

Do **not** add more generic kitchen/bathroom sentences. Instead, where a city's `local_body` already names
a housing characteristic (older homes, newer builds, specific neighborhoods), extend the kitchen/bathroom
bullets with **one city-specific clause** tied to that fact. This deepens secondary intent *and* uniqueness
in the same edit, without competing with the dedicated service pages.
