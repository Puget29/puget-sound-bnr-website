Kitchen remodeling in the Puget Sound area

A kitchen remodel can be as focused as new cabinets and countertops or as complete as a new layout. We talk through your space and your goals in the free consultation and recommend the scope that fits.

What we offer

  • Kitchen layouts and reconfiguration
  • Cabinets, new or refaced
  • Countertops
  • Flooring
  • Backsplash, fixtures, lighting and finishes

Options and materials

Cabinet styles and colors, countertop materials, tile backsplashes and flooring types are all part of the conversation. We help you compare the options for your style, your budget and the way you use the kitchen.

What to expect

You can expect a free consultation, a plan you understand before work begins, clear communication from the first call to the final walkthrough, and a finished kitchen we are proud to hand over.
