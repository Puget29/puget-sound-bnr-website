Privacy policy

What we collect

When you use a form on this site we collect what you type: your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, a description of your project and, if you add them, photos of your space.

How we use it

We use these details to reply to your request and to plan your project with you. We do not sell your details.

Who sees it

The BNR office team sees your message. We share details only with the people who need them to do the work you asked for, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as we need them to answer you and to work on your project, then we delete them.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the details you sent. Call (425) 696-4004 or write to Office@pugetsoundbnr.com.
