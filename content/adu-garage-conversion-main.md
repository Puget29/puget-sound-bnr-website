## ADU and Garage Conversion FAQs

### What is the difference between an ADU, a DADU and a garage conversion?

An ADU, or accessory dwelling unit, is a secondary living space on your property. A DADU is the detached version, a standalone unit built separately from the main house, often in the backyard. A garage conversion turns an existing garage into finished living space. We help you weigh which fits your lot and goals in the free consultation.

### What can I use an ADU or converted garage for?

Almost any living use. Many Bellevue homeowners create space for family or returning relatives, a private guest suite, a home office or studio, or a unit they plan to rent. How you want to use it shapes the layout and finishes, so we start there.

### What affects how long an ADU or conversion takes?

Timing depends on whether you are converting a garage or building a detached unit, how much structural and systems work is involved, and the finishes chosen. A straightforward conversion moves faster than a new detached unit. We talk through a realistic sequence with you during planning.

### What affects the cost of an ADU or garage conversion?

Cost is driven by the scope of work, whether the unit is attached or detached, the systems the space needs and the finishes you choose. Because every property is different, we review these factors with you in the free consultation rather than quoting a figure before we understand the project.

### Do you help with the layout and design?

Yes. We work through the layout and the material choices with you, explaining what each option involves so you can make decisions with real information. The goal is a space that fits how you will actually use it, not just how it looks.

### Do ADU and garage conversion projects need permits?

Many of these projects require permits, particularly when they involve structural changes or new plumbing and electrical. Requirements vary by property, so we discuss what your specific project involves during planning rather than making assumptions.

### Is a garage conversion or a detached ADU better for my property?

It depends on your lot, your budget and how you want to use the space. Converting an existing garage can be a more contained project, while a detached unit offers more separation and flexibility. We help you compare both in the consultation.
