## What to Include in Your Message

The more we know, the more useful our first conversation is. If you can, tell us:

- **What you want done.** A kitchen, a bathroom, a roof repair, a deck, an ADU, siding, flooring, painting, or something you are not sure how to describe.
- **Where the home is.** The city or neighborhood, so we can confirm we serve your area.
- **What bothers you today.** A leak, a layout that does not work, worn floors, an outdated look. It helps us understand the real problem.
- **Your timing.** Whether you are ready to start soon or planning for later.
- **A rough budget range, if you have one.** It helps us suggest options that fit. If you do not know yet, that is fine.
- **Photos or ideas.** Pictures of the space, or examples you like, make the conversation much faster.

1. **We follow up.** We reach out to talk through your project and set up a time to visit.
2. **We visit your home.** We look at the space, listen to your goals and discuss options and a realistic budget range. This visit is free.
3. **You get a written estimate.** We send a clear scope and estimate after the visit, so you can decide with the full picture.

## Getting Ready for Your Consultation

You do not need to prepare much, but a little thought up front makes the visit more useful.

- **Walk through the space.** Notice what works and what does not. Who uses it, and how?
- **Collect ideas.** Save photos, links or notes about styles and features you like.
- **Think about priorities.** What matters most: layout, durability, appearance, budget, timeline?
- **Gather paperwork if you have it.** Old plans, inspection reports or earlier quotes can help us understand the home.
- **Have the decision makers there.** If more than one person has a say, it saves time when everyone can join the visit.
