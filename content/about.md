## About Puget Sound BNR

Puget Sound Builders and Renovation, known as Puget Sound BNR, is a general contractor based in Bellevue, Washington. We design, repair and build for homeowners in Seattle, Bellevue and the surrounding Puget Sound communities, from a single leaking roof to a full kitchen renovation.

We have more than 5 years of experience building and renovating homes in this area, and we have learned what this climate and these houses ask of a contractor. Wet winters, older homes with surprises behind the walls, and city permit rules that change from one town to the next all call for the same thing: a clear plan, honest advice and careful work.

Our approach is simple. We listen first, we tell you plainly what your home needs, and we put the plan in writing before any work starts. Then one team builds it, keeps you informed and walks the finished work with you.

## How We Work

Every home and every homeowner is different, but the way we run a project stays the same.

- **A free, honest consultation.** We visit your home, look at the space and listen. You leave with a clear idea of your options, including what can wait and where you can save, even when that means a smaller project.
- **A plan in writing.** You get a written scope of work and an estimate, so you know what the project covers before it starts.
- **One team.** Design, planning, construction and the final walkthrough are handled together, so nothing falls between trades and you are not chasing anyone.
- **Clear communication.** You know who is on site, what is happening this week and what comes next. If something unexpected shows up behind a wall, we explain it, show you the options and agree on the next step with you.
- **A clean, respectful job site.** We protect your home, clean up at the end of every work day and treat your house the way we would treat our own.
- **A final walkthrough.** We walk the finished work with you, check every detail against the plan, and fix anything that is not right before we call the project complete.

## What We Believe

### Honest advice comes first

The best thing a contractor can do is tell you the truth. Sometimes a repair is enough, and sometimes a replacement is smarter. Sometimes a smaller project gives you most of the benefit. We would rather earn your trust with an honest answer than win a bigger job with a pushy one.

### Planning prevents problems

Most remodeling frustrations come from surprises: unclear scope, late decisions, missing permits and materials that arrive late. We reduce them by planning early, ordering long lead items in time and talking through decisions before they hold up the work.

### The hidden work matters most

Waterproofing behind tile, flashing around windows, footings under a deck, the weather barrier behind siding. The parts of a project you never see again are the parts that decide how long it lasts, so that is where we put our attention.

### Your home is not a template

We design and build around your house, your habits and your budget. A kitchen for a family that cooks every night is not the same as one for someone who entertains, and a roof on a shaded lot is not the same as a roof in full sun.

## Built for the Pacific Northwest

Building in Puget Sound is different from building in a dry climate. We plan for it on every project.

- **Rain and moisture.** Long wet seasons test roofs, siding, decks and bathrooms. We pay close attention to drainage, flashing, ventilation and waterproofing.
- **Older homes.** Many Seattle area homes are decades old. Walls can hide outdated wiring, old leaks or materials that need special handling, so we plan a contingency into projects where it makes sense.
- **Permits and local rules.** Seattle, Bellevue and other nearby cities each have their own requirements. We check what applies to your project in the planning stage and help you with the permit requirements.
- **The weather calendar.** Exterior work depends on the weather. We plan around the forecast and tell you plainly if it changes the schedule.
