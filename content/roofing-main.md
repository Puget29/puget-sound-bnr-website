- **Name the real problem.** Active leaks, missing shingles, aging material or damage that keeps coming back.

## Signs It May Be Time to Replace Your Roof

Not every roof needs replacing. These are the signs that usually point to a replacement, rather than to another round of patch repairs.

- **Leaks keep returning.** Water finds its way in again soon after each repair.
- **Shingles are failing.** Pieces are cracked, curling, missing or shedding grit into the gutters.
- **Stains appear inside.** Ceilings or attic boards show water marks after heavy Eastside rain.
- **The roof is simply old.** The covering is near the end of its expected life.
- **Daylight or damp in the attic.** You can see light through the deck or feel persistent moisture above.
- **Sagging or soft spots.** The roofline dips or feels soft underfoot, which points to deeper trouble.

If several of these sound familiar, a free consultation is a low-pressure way to find out what is realistic for your roof and priorities. This is especially common on older Eastside homes whose roofs have weathered many wet winters.

## Roofing FAQs

### Do I need a roof repair or a full replacement?

It depends on the roof's age and condition. A sound roof with a single weak area often needs only a repair, while a roof that leaks in several places or is near the end of its life usually calls for replacement. In your free consultation we look at the roof and talk through which makes sense.

### What affects how long a roof replacement takes?

Timing depends on the size and pitch of the roof, the material chosen, the condition of the deck beneath and the weather. A straightforward replacement moves faster than one that uncovers repairs underneath. We talk through a realistic sequence with you during planning.

### What affects the cost of a new roof?

Cost is driven by the size of the roof, the material you choose, how much of the deck or flashing needs work and the complexity of the roofline. Because every roof is different, we review these factors with you in the free consultation rather than quoting a figure before we understand the project.

### Which roofing material is best for a Bellevue home?

There is no single best choice. Materials differ in look, longevity and how they shed our steady rain, so the right one depends on your home, your priorities and your budget. We explain the trade-offs so you can decide with real information rather than guesswork.

### Can you repair a leak without replacing the whole roof?

Often, yes. When the wider roof is sound and the leak traces to a specific spot, such as worn flashing or a few failed shingles, a targeted repair can solve it. If leaks keep returning across the roof, we talk through whether replacement is the more sensible path.

### Will a roof replacement disrupt my household?

A replacement is active work, but most of it happens outside. We prepare and protect the areas around your home, keep you informed about who is on site, and plan the timing with you so daily life keeps moving as much as possible.

### Do roofing projects need permits in Bellevue?

Many roof replacements require a permit, and the details vary by project and by the age of the home. Older Eastside homes can also bring conditions worth checking early. We discuss what your specific roof involves during planning rather than making assumptions.
