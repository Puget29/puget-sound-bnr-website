## How to Look at a Finished Project

When you look at photos of remodeling work, a few things tell you more than the style does.

- **Clean lines and edges.** Look at where tile meets the wall, where paint meets trim and where cabinets meet the ceiling. Tight, even lines are the sign of careful work.
- **How the room works.** A beautiful space that is hard to use is not a good remodel. Look for sensible layouts, enough storage and good lighting.
- **Details that match.** Trim, fixtures, hardware and finishes should look like they belong together.
- **Signs of planning.** Outlets in the right places, proper ventilation, balanced lighting and smooth transitions between rooms all come from planning before building.
- **How it will age.** Materials chosen for the room, such as water resistant surfaces in a bathroom, hold up better over time.

## How Every Project Goes

Whatever the service, the way we run a project follows the same four steps.

1. **Consultation.** We visit your home, listen to your goals and look at the space. It is free, and there is no pressure.
2. **Planning and design.** We turn your ideas into a written scope, choose materials with you and identify the permits your project needs.
3. **Construction.** One team handles the work in a sensible order, keeps you updated and protects your home along the way.
4. **Final walkthrough.** We check every detail with you and fix anything that is not right before we call the project complete.

Photos of finished work are a great starting point for your own ideas.

- **Bring them to the consultation.** Pictures help us understand your taste quickly, and they save time.

- **Ask questions.** If you see something in a photo you like, ask us what it involves. We will give you an honest answer, including what it costs in time and effort.
