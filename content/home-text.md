## BLOCK 3: How we work
### A Clear Process From the First Call to the Final Walkthrough

A project goes well when the steps are clear and nothing is a surprise.

1. **Free consultation.** We visit your home, look at the space and listen to your goals, your ideas and your budget. There is no pressure.
2. **Planning and design.** We turn your ideas into a written scope of work, choose materials with you and identify the permits your project needs.
3. **Construction.** One team handles the work in a sensible order, keeps you updated and protects your home along the way.
4. **Final walkthrough.** We check every detail with you and fix anything that is not right before we call the project complete.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### What kinds of projects do you do?

We handle kitchen and bathroom remodeling, roofing, deck building, ADU and garage conversions, siding, flooring and painting. If you are not sure which service your project falls under, call us and we will help you work it out.

#### Is the consultation really free?

Yes. We visit your home, look at the project and talk through your options at no cost and with no pressure. Afterward we follow up with a written estimate.

#### How much will my project cost?

It depends on the size, the materials and how much work is involved. The fastest way to get a real number is a free consultation, where we look at your home and give you a clear written estimate.

#### How long will my project take?

It depends on the scope. Planning, design and permits come first, and then construction. After your consultation we give you a written schedule for your specific project.

#### Do you help with permits?

Yes. We check what permits your project needs during the planning stage and help you with the permit requirements. Rules differ between Seattle, Bellevue and other cities, so we look at the rules for your property.

#### Which areas do you serve?

We serve Seattle, Bellevue and the surrounding Puget Sound communities. Tell us where you are and we will confirm we can take on your project.
